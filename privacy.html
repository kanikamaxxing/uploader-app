Privacy policy

This is a personal tool operated by its single owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

  • It accesses Google user data only through the YouTube Data API, with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl, and only for the owner's own account.
  • The OAuth tokens it obtains are stored on the owner's private server and used solely to upload and manage videos on that channel.
  • No data about any other person is collected, stored, shared, or sold. The tool has no third-party users.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google account permissions.
